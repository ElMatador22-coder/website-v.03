What Salesloft and Clari are built for

Sales engagement plus the forecast roll-up.

For account teams, Salesloft lists Cadence for multi-step outreach, Conversation Intelligence, Deals and Rhythm. For revenue leaders it lists Clari Forecast, Inspect and Analytics, plus AI agents and signals across the suite.

Clari Forecast describes itself as a way to predict and manage revenue, with automated roll-ups, views from the company total down to single deals, and scenario modeling. It names sales leaders and revenue operations as its users and integrates with Salesforce.

If your priority is running high-volume outbound sequences and rolling a forecast up through a large sales organization, that is the center of what this suite does.

What SalesOgre AI is built for

The rep's next move on a live deal.

SalesOgre AI starts from the deal, not the sequence. It maps every stakeholder against MEDDPICC, reads who is a champion, a supporter, nervous or a blocker, and shows the sentence behind each read. The forecast is scored on what the buyer did: who replied, who went quiet, which commitments were kept.

Outreach still happens. SalesOgre AI drafts the email, the sequence or the meeting proposal, and nothing goes out until a person approves it.

How to choose

Match the tool to the gap.

Salesloft and Clari are likely the better fit if

You run a large sales organization where the main job is volume outbound at scale and rolling a forecast up through many managers. You want engagement, conversation recording and forecasting from one vendor, and you are comfortable buying through a sales conversation.

SalesOgre AI is likely the better fit if

Your pipeline has activity but deals still stall in the middle, because nobody is sure who the champion is or whether the economic buyer is engaged. You want each rep coached on the deal itself, with every outbound message approved by a person, and you want to start one seller at a published price.

Questions

Common questions. Straight answers.

Is Clari part of Salesloft now?

Salesloft's website lists Clari Forecast as one of its products, under Revenue Intelligence, and clari.com's pricing page redirects to salesloft.com. Treat them as one vendor when you evaluate.

Do Salesloft and Clari publish their pricing?

No prices are published on Salesloft's site; it points buyers to Talk to Sales. SalesOgre AI publishes its prices: Free at $0, the Individual License at $59 a month on a 12-month term, and the Individual Annual Commit at $1,000 a year. Enterprise, for teams of 5 or more, is priced per team and contract.

How is SalesOgre AI's forecast different from Clari Forecast?

Clari Forecast focuses on roll-ups, scenario modeling and views from the company total down to single deals. SalesOgre AI scores each deal on buyer-side evidence, such as who replied, who went quiet and which commitments were kept, and only advances a stage on a buyer commitment.

Does SalesOgre AI run outbound sequences?

SalesOgre AI drafts emails, sequences and meeting proposals for the rep. Nothing is sent until a person approves it.
