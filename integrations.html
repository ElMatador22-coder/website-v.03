Questions

Common questions. Straight answers.

Which CRMs does SalesOgre work with?

Salesforce and HubSpot.

Does SalesOgre connect to email and calendar?

Yes. Gmail, Outlook and your calendar feed the deal picture, so notes, threads and meetings land on the right deal without retyping.

Does SalesOgre send email from my account on its own?

No. It drafts the email or the meeting proposal and puts it in front of you. Nothing goes out until you approve it.

Is CRM sync included in every plan?

CRM sync is listed under the Enterprise plan on the Pricing page, along with SSO, audit and per-org isolation.
