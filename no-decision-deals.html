Why deals end in no decision and how to catch them early.

Losing to a competitor hurts. Losing to the status quo hurts more, because the deal usually died slowly while the forecast still called it healthy.

What no decision is

The buyer chose nothing.

A no-decision loss is a deal where the buyer does not choose any vendor. The project stalls or quietly disappears.

It rarely looks like a loss while it is happening. Meetings still get booked while the close date moves one more month.

Why it happens

The cost of doing nothing stayed too low.

Most no-decision losses trace back to a short list of causes. The most common is a problem that was never put in the buyer's own numbers, so doing nothing always looked affordable.

Questions

Common questions. Straight answers.

What does no decision mean in sales?

The buyer does not choose any vendor. The project stalls or is dropped, and the deal ends without a competitor winning it.

Why do deals end in no decision?

Usually because the cost of doing nothing never got big enough, or because the economic buyer was never engaged.

How do you spot a stalled deal early?

Watch for missed commitments, a champion going quiet, close dates that move without a buyer-side reason, and no new stakeholders joining over several weeks.

How does SalesOgre help with stalled deals?

It scores deals on buyer events, flags when a champion goes quiet or a commitment slips, and drafts the next best action for you to review and approve.
