Deal intelligence software. What it should actually tell you.

Deal intelligence software reads the activity around an opportunity and tells you whether the deal is real. The good ones work from what the buyer did. The rest count what the seller did and call it insight.

The definition

A read on the deal, from the buyer's side.

Deal intelligence pulls together the emails, meetings, notes and CRM history on an opportunity and turns them into a judgment: is this deal moving, who matters, and what happens next. It sits between the system of record, which stores what happened, and the rep, who has to decide what to do about it.

The test of any deal intelligence tool is simple. When it says a deal is healthy, can it show you the buyer behavior that proves it?

Why it matters

Most lost deals never meet a competitor.

Research behind The JOLT Effect by Matt Dixon and Ted McKenna found that 40 to 60 percent of lost deals end in no decision. The buyer just stops. A tool that tracks your activity will tell you the deal looks busy right up to the day it dies.

Deal intelligence earns its keep by catching that drift early: a champion who goes quiet, a stakeholder who never shows up, a date that keeps moving.

  • Seller activity tells you effort.
  • Buyer behavior tells you the deal.

Questions

Common questions. Straight answers.

What is deal intelligence software?

Software that reads the emails, meetings, notes and CRM history on an opportunity and tells you whether the deal is moving, who matters and what to do next.

How is deal intelligence different from conversation intelligence?

Conversation intelligence records and analyzes calls. Deal intelligence looks across every interaction on an opportunity, calls included, to judge the deal as a whole.

What signals should deal intelligence track?

Buyer-side signals: who replied and who went quiet, which commitments were kept, whether the champion is taking action internally, and which members of the buying committee have never engaged.

Does SalesOgre AI replace my CRM?

No. Salesforce or HubSpot stays the system of record. SalesOgre AI reads from it, keeps notes and fields current, and gives the rep a read on each deal.
