The operating loop

Every signal ends in executed work.

Capture: the data foundation

Direct integrations to Salesforce, HubSpot, Gmail, Outlook and calendar assemble one living picture of every deal. Meeting notes, emails, and transcripts become structured deal intelligence. Stakeholders are extracted, roles mapped, evidence logged. Nobody types.

Read: committee & momentum

Buying-committee mapping against MEDDPICC. Stakeholder state reads (nervous, blocker, champion, supporter) from real communication patterns. Timeline Confidence and Honest Quota scoring that tells you what the pipeline actually is, whatever everyone hopes it is. How MEDDPICC deal scoring works → How to spot a real champion →

Coach: front of stage & behind the scenes

Stage-by-stage and meeting-by-meeting: pre-meeting briefs with the one objective that counts, post-meeting truth on whether you got an advance or a polite continuation, and a recalibration every time a note lands. Honest, cheery, relentless about the next commitment.

Act: the play, already run

Next best actions execute: de-risk emails drafted, champion toolkits generated, exec-alignment sequences built, meetings proposed on both calendars, CRM updated to perfect hygiene. Human approval on everything outbound. You're the seller. The platform is the crew.
