What Gong is built for

The recorded record of every conversation.

Gong says it records and transcribes calls, emails and meetings, then layers pipeline inspection, forecasting, engagement, enablement and coaching tools on top. It names sales, marketing, customer success, enablement and revenue operations as its users.

If your leadership team wants a searchable library of what was said on every customer call, across hundreds of reps, that is the center of what Gong does.

What SalesOgre is built for

The rep's next move on a live deal.

SalesOgre starts from the account executive's open deals. It maps every stakeholder against MEDDPICC, reads who is a champion, a supporter, nervous or a blocker, and shows you the sentence behind each read. Before a meeting the rep gets one objective that counts. After it, an honest read on whether the buyer committed to anything.

Then it drafts the follow-up, the sequence or the meeting proposal. Nothing goes out until a person approves it. The coaching layer was written by people who carried a quota.

  • Stage changes only on a buyer-side commitment.
  • Forecast scored on what the buyer did, such as who replied and who went quiet.

How to choose

Match the tool to the gap.

Gong is likely the better fit if

You run a large revenue organization and the priority is capturing and searching every customer call. You want Zoom, Teams, Meet or Webex recording, Microsoft Dynamics support, and a vendor-led implementation over several weeks. You are comfortable buying through a custom proposal.

SalesOgre is likely the better fit if

Your deals stall in the middle because nobody is sure who the champion is or whether the economic buyer is engaged. You want each rep coached on the deal itself, with every outbound message approved by a person. You want one seller to start at a published price and the team to follow on Enterprise.

Questions

Common questions. Straight answers.

What is the main difference between SalesOgre and Gong?

Gong describes itself as a revenue AI platform that records and transcribes calls, emails and meetings for the whole revenue team. SalesOgre is built around the account executive's deals: it reads every deal against MEDDPICC, coaches the rep before, during and after meetings, and drafts the next action for the rep to approve.

Does Gong publish its pricing?

No. Gong's FAQ says it prices through a consultative approach with customized proposals based on users, modules and integrations, and asks prospects to speak with a Gong representative. SalesOgre publishes its individual prices: Free at $0, the Individual License at $59 a month on a 12-month term, and the Individual Annual Commit at $1,000 a year. Enterprise, for teams of 5 or more, is priced per team and contract.

Which CRMs do SalesOgre and Gong connect to?

SalesOgre connects to Salesforce and HubSpot, plus Gmail, Outlook and your calendar. Gong's FAQ names Salesforce, HubSpot and Microsoft Dynamics, along with Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet and Webex for calls.

Does SalesOgre send emails without approval?

No. SalesOgre drafts the email, the sequence or the meeting proposal and puts it in front of the rep. Nothing goes out until a person approves it.

When is Gong the better choice?

When your main need is a recorded, searchable library of every customer call across a large revenue organization, with video and telephony integrations and a dedicated implementation team, Gong is built for that job.
