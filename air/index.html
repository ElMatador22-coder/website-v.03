Why AI-R

A regulator, not another proxy.

We built AI-R because we needed it. Running our own AI products, the cost curve was the product risk: every feature that worked made the bill worse, and every dashboard we tried told us about it a month late. A gateway that only forwards calls doesn't fix that. Something has to decide, verify, reuse, and refuse.

That's a regulator. It's the same idea as the dam: hold the reservoir, release exactly what's needed, and put the rest to work. It runs across providers by design, so the cheapest capable model always wins and no vendor owns your margin.
