One objective that counts.

Most meeting prep is a pile of facts. Useful prep names the one commitment this meeting has to produce and who in the room can give it to you.

SalesOgre builds a pre-meeting brief with the one objective that counts, so the rep walks in knowing what an advance looks like.

Questions

Common questions. Straight answers.

What is AI sales coaching?

Software that reviews a rep's deals and conversations and suggests what to do next, before a meeting, during it or after it. The useful kind works from the deal's evidence and leaves the decision with the rep.

Does AI sales coaching replace sales managers?

No. It gives managers and reps a consistent read on every deal so coaching time goes to judgment calls, and the rep still decides what to say and send.

When does SalesOgre coach?

Before each meeting with a brief and the one objective that counts, during live calls with short cues, and after each meeting with an honest read on whether the deal advanced.

Does SalesOgre send anything without approval?

No. It drafts emails, sequences and meeting proposals for you to review. Nothing goes out until you approve it.
