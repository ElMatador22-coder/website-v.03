The basics

What it is and who it is for.

What is SalesOgre AI?

SalesOgre AI is a revenue system built into sales execution. It reads every deal in real time, coaches the account executive before and after each meeting, and drafts the next best action for the rep to approve. You stay the seller.

Who is SalesOgre built for?

Account executives and the sales leaders who run them at B2B companies selling into mid-market and enterprise buyers. It was built by salespeople who carried a quota, for the parts of the job that never show up in a CRM field.

Does SalesOgre replace my CRM?

It replaces the time you spend feeding your CRM. Notes, fields and follow-ups are captured and updated for you, so your system of record stays current without Friday data entry.

What does SalesOgre integrate with?

Salesforce and HubSpot on the CRM side, Gmail and Outlook for email, and your calendar. Notes, emails and meetings from those tools feed one picture of each deal. The platform page covers how that data is used.

The product

How it works on a live deal.

Does SalesOgre send emails or messages on its own?

No. SalesOgre drafts the email, the sequence or the meeting proposal and puts it in front of you. Nothing goes out until you approve it. Every send and every dial is started by a person.

How does SalesOgre tell a real champion from someone being polite?

It reads the communication patterns in your notes, emails and calls and classifies each stakeholder as a champion, supporter, nervous stakeholder or blocker. It also shows you the sentence that led to the read, so you can judge it yourself. The full guide covers four tests for a real champion.

How does SalesOgre use MEDDPICC?

Every deal is mapped against MEDDPICC and scored on what the buyer actually did, such as who replied, who went quiet and which commitments were kept. A stage only advances on a buyer-side commitment. The full breakdown is on the MEDDPICC deal scoring page.

What is AI-R?

AI-R is the AI regulator that SalesOgre runs on. It meters every model call, routes each job to the cheapest model that passes a quality bar, and enforces spending caps. That is what lets SalesOgre be priced like software.

Pricing and getting started.

How much does SalesOgre cost?

Free is $0 for 5 prospects, 5 deals and 5 people, with no AI work. The Individual License is $59 a month on a 12-month term, and the Individual Annual Commit is $1,000 a year, paid upfront. Enterprise has custom pricing for teams of 5 or more; email cj@salesogre.com. Every license payment goes into your AI Wallet.

What is the AI Wallet?

The balance that pays for Charlie's AI work, such as research, meeting coaching and drafts. Every license payment goes into it, and unused balance carries over. A card on file tops it up automatically when it drops below $52, by the amount you choose, $100 or more.

What happens in a demo?

Twenty minutes, no slides. You paste in notes from a real deal, or we use ours. The platform reads the room and shows the evidence, then we run a live call so you can watch the coaching land mid-conversation. Then you tell us where it got it wrong.
