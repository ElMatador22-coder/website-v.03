How to map a buying committee before it maps you.

A lot of stalled deals had a buying committee the seller never fully saw. Here is how to map who decides, who influences and who is missing from the room.

Why it matters

One contact is not a deal.

Enterprise purchases rarely come down to one person. The economic buyer releases the money. Legal, security and procurement can slow it down or stop it.

If you only know one or two of them, your forecast is built on a partial view. The deal can look healthy right up to the moment a person you never met says no.

Questions

Common questions. Straight answers.

What is a buying committee in B2B sales?

The group of people inside the buying company who influence or approve the purchase. It usually includes the economic buyer and the people who will use the product, plus reviewers such as legal, security and procurement.

How do you map a buying committee?

List everyone who has touched the deal, note the role each one plays and how they are behaving toward you, then look for the roles nobody is covering yet. The gaps matter more than the names you already have.

Who is usually missing from the buying committee?

The economic buyer and the risk reviewers. Deals often run for months with users and a champion while the person who signs and the people who can say no have never been in a meeting.
